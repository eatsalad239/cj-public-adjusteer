# CRITICAL COMPLIANCE NOTES - CJ CLAIM SERVICES

## ⚠️ PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES FOR PUBLIC ADJUSTERS

### CANNOT PAY REFERRAL FEES TO:
- ❌ Roofing contractors
- ❌ General contractors  
- ❌ Restoration companies
- ❌ Any unlicensed individuals or entities

This is STRICTLY PROHIBITED by Louisiana insurance regulations.
Violations can result in:
- License suspension or revocation
- Fines and penalties
- Legal action

## ✅ WHAT IS ALLOWED

### Professional Partnerships:
- Working cooperatively with contractors
- Each party bills independently for their services
- Contractors handle repairs at their standard rates
- Public adjusters handle claims on contingency fee basis (paid by policyholder)
- Educational collaboration and resource sharing

### The Value Proposition (No Money Changes Hands):
1. **For Contractors:**
   - They get to keep their customers
   - They avoid Act 144 violations
   - They can focus on repair work
   - Their customers get professional claims help
   - They maintain customer relationships

2. **For CJ Claim Services:**
   - Receive client referrals (but don't pay for them)
   - Charge standard contingency fee to policyholder (not contractor)
   - Build professional network
   - Help more property owners

## 📝 KEY MESSAGING POINTS

### CORRECT Language:
- "Professional partnership"
- "We work together to serve customers"
- "Each party operates independently"
- "Contractors focus on repairs, we handle claims"
- "No fees between professionals"

### INCORRECT Language (Never Use):
- "Referral fees"
- "Commissions"  
- "Get paid for referrals"
- "Earn money by sending clients"
- "Revenue sharing"
