## 💼 How the Partnership Works (Legally)

1. **Contractor** meets customer with property damage
2. **Contractor** cannot handle insurance (Act 144)
3. **Contractor** refers customer to CJ Claim Services
4. **CJ** contracts directly with customer (contingency fee)
5. **Contractor** contracts directly with customer (repairs)
6. **CJ** refers customers needing repairs to contractor
7. **Both serve** same customer independently
8. **NO MONEY** exchanged between CJ and contractor
