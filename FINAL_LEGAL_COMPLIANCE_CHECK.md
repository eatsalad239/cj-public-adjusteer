## ⚖️ Louisiana-Specific Compliance

### Public Adjusters in Louisiana:
- **CAN**: Document, evaluate, prepare, present, and process claims
- **CAN**: Communicate with insurance companies about claims
- **CAN**: Advise on coverage and policy interpretation
- **CAN**: Work on contingency fee basis (10% max by law)
- **CANNOT**: "Negotiate" (considered practicing law)
- **CANNOT**: Provide legal advice
- **CANNOT**: Pay referral fees to unlicensed persons
